## English

**Licence.** The plugin is free, open-source software under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE), provided "as is", without
warranty (sections 7–8 of the licence).

**Not legal advice.** The plugin helps a client and their advocate organise a case, find law and case law, and
draft documents. It gives no legal advice, does not replace an advocate and creates no lawyer-client
relationship with the author. A person checks every conclusion, deadline and document, and only a person signs
and files. Deadlines are computed conservatively and marked provisional until an advocate confirms them.

**Sources.** Law texts are open data of the Verkhovna Rada of Ukraine under CC BY 4.0. Supreme Court positions
and court decisions come from state registers with their own rules. The plugin queries them in a targeted way,
with pauses and no bulk downloading, and stops at a CAPTCHA or block page. You agree not to use it otherwise,
including any attempt to identify people anonymised in decisions (`ОСОБА_N`). The author does not guarantee the
sources' availability or completeness: an empty result means "not found", not "does not exist".

extent the law allows, the author is not liable for harm arising from use of the plugin.
